Задание 3
Алгоритм работы

Обрати внимание, что это задание воспринимается полностью на слух, перед тобой не будет транскрипта

Время ответа на каждый вопрос – 40 секунд

1. Пока интервьюер говорит, тебе нужно уловить основную тему интервью

2. Вспомнить основную лексику по этой теме

3. Если вопрос про что-то самое лучшее/любимое/известное следуй схеме ответа

a. Вводная фраза Actually there are a lot of exciting Russian writers…/You know, there are a lot of…/If you ask me, there are a lot of famous sportsmen…

b. Пример For instance Dostoevsky/Yashin/Gogol/Kharlamov

c. Самый лучший/любимый/интересный … But my favourite Russian sportsman is Lev Yashin/But most of all I like a park in my hometown

4. Ответить минимум двумя предложениями на вопрос

Например:

Interviewer: Hello everybody! It’s Teenagers Round the World Channel. Our guest today is a teenager from Russia and we are going to discuss teenagers’ attitude to local places of interest. We’d like to know our guest’s point of view on this issue. Please answer five questions. So, let’s get started.

Interviewer: What are some of the places of interest in your region? Why should a tourist see them?

Student: Actually, there are many places of interest in my region, for instance, a giant metallurgical plant, resorts in the Ural Mountains and thermal springs. Tourists should see them because tourists will get an unforgettable experience and beautiful photos.

Interviewer: Is it important to preserve historical places? Why or why not?

Student: I’m sure it’s important to preserve historical places because people should be connected with their roots. Moreover, historical places are vital for our culture.

Interviewer: What is your favourite place in your home town? Why do you like it?

Student: As for me, my favourite place in my home town is one district with wonderful old architecture. I like it because I grew up in the district and got really interested in architecture.

Interviewer: What sort of places will be interesting for people in the future? Why do you think so?

Student: I think, people will be interested in history at any time. They will find our flats and houses both familiar and weird as we find some houses of the 15th century.

Interviewer: How can we make teenagers be more interested in local culture?

Student: In my opinion, local culture should be shown to teenagers in a fascinating and entertaining way. For instance, videogames and excursions in virtual reality will be definitely interesting for them.

Interviewer: Thank you very much for your interview.

Русские спортсмены
