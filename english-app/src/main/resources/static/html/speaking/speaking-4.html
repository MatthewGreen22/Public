Задание 4
Алгоритм работы

Task 4. Imagine that you and your friend are doing a school project “Sports activities”. You have found some photos to illustrate it but for technical reasons you cannot send them now. Leave a voice message to your friend explaining your choice of the photos and sharing some ideas about the project. In 2.5 minutes be ready to:

• explain the choice of the illustrations for the project by briefly describing them and noting the differences;

• mention the advantages (1–2) of the two sports activities;

• mention the disadvantages (1–2) of the two sports activities;

• express your opinion on the subject of the project – which of the sports activities presented in the pictures you’d prefer and why.

You will speak for not more than 3 minutes (12–15 sentences). You have to talk continuously.

От тебя требуется

• Объяснить выбор фотографий

• Коротко их описать

• Обозначить различия между ними

• Упомянуть преимущества двух видов спорта

• Упомянуть недостатки двух видов спорта

• Выразить свое мнение, какой из представленных на фотографиях видов спорта ты бы предпочел/предпочла и почему

У тебя есть две с половиной минуты на подготовку и три минуты на ответ. При ответе сказать НЕ меньше 12-ти предложений

Подчеркиванием обозначены клише, которые нужно просто запомнить

Это задание повышенной сложности, поэтому тебе нужно показать знание сложных грамматических конструкций

1. Обрати внимание на название проекта – в нашем случае “Sports activities”

2. Начинай с одного и того же вступления

“Hello, «имя твоего друга»! Guess what! I've just found two photos for our project "Sports activities" and I'd like to discuss them with you.”

3. Переходи к описанию первой фотографии
Можно выразить предположение, потому что ты не знаешь наверняка – “In the first picture there is a man playing football. Probably, he is training.”

4. Опиши вторую фотографию
Также можно добавить предположение – “In the second picture, there is a girl in a swimming pool. She might be training as well.”

5. Объясняем выбор фотографий
В нашем случае “The photos are really suitable for the project as they illustrate contrasting types of sports activities.”

6. Переход к различиям
В нашем случае нужно указать различия в видах спорта – team/ individual sport; indoor/ outdoor sport; “Though the photos have something in common, there are some differences between them. To begin with, in the first photo the man is doing an outdoor sports activity contrary to the girl in the second picture – she’s indoors”

7. Второе различие
“Moreover, there are two types of sports activities. The first photo shows a team sport while the other photo shows an individual sport.”

8. Рассказать про преимущества
“You know, these types of sports activities have some advantages. Team sports develop your communication skills. However, in individual sports you don’t depend on anyone, so no one can let you down.”

9. Рассказать про недостатки
“As for the disadvantages of the types of sports activities mentioned before, team sports often involve quarrels and argues. As for individual sports, you can’t rely on anyone but yourself.”

10. Выразить свое мнение
“If you ask me, I would prefer football. You know, I’m keen on team sports. It gives me the opportunity to interact with my teammates and go for the win all together.”

11. Заключение
“That's all I've been meaning to say on the matter. I hope you like the photos. Bye.”

Пример ответа:

• “Hello, Ben! Guess what! I've just found two photos for our project "Sports activities" and I'd like to discuss them with you.

• In the first picture there is a man playing football. Probably, he is training. In the second picture, there is a girl in a swimming pool. She might be training as well.

• The photos are really suitable for the project as they illustrate contrasting types of sports activities.

• Though the photos have something in common, there are some differences between them. To begin with, in the first photo the man is doing an outdoor sports activity contrary to the girl in the second picture – she’s indoors. Moreover, there are two types of sports activities. The first photo shows a team sport while the other photo shows an individual sport.

• You know, these types of sports activities have some advantages. Team sports develop your communication skills. However, in individual sports you don’t depend on anyone, so no one can let you down.

• As for the disadvantages of the types of sports activities mentioned before, team sports often involve quarrels and argues. As for individual sports, you can’t rely on anyone but yourself.

• If you ask me, I would prefer football. You know, I’m keen on team sports. It gives me the opportunity to interact with my teammates and go for the win all together.

• That's all I've been meaning to say on the matter. I hope you like the photos. Bye.

Никаких You can see/we can see…

Используй слова логической связи – however, on the one hand … on the other hand…, as for…, speaking of…

Различия должны касаться темы проекта. Не нужно сравнивать, например, цвета футболок и мебели

Очень важно отвечать в соответствии с последним пунктом – в нашем случае тебе нужно сказать, какой из видов спорта на фото ты бы предпочел/предпочла и почему

Если в последнем пункте “you’d prefer” – отвечаем “I would prefer”;
если “prefer” – “I prefer”;
если “preferred as a child” – отвечаем “when I was a child, I preferred…”;
если “would like” – отвечаем “I would like”;
если “would enjoy” – “I would enjoy”;
“whether you believe using a boat is better or not.” – “I believe using a boat is/isn’t better…
